Gel is a polish-like colour cured under a lamp: glossy, flexible and usually the gentlest on natural nails. Dip builds colour from pigment powder over a bonding base, sets without a lamp and tends to be harder-wearing. Acrylic is a liquid-and-powder mix sculpted onto the nail, the strongest option for adding real length. Draft notes

Before this goes live: things to confirm with the owner

Everything on this page comes from what the shop or public listings already say. Where sources disagree or say nothing, the slot is marked rather than guessed. This list is every one of those slots.

  • Hours. Fresha, Birdeye, Wheree and Apple Maps list Mon–Fri 10–7, Sat 10–6, Sun 11–6. Yelp and YellowPages snippets show Mon–Sat 10–6 with no Sunday. The shop's own posts give no hours. Pills sit on the Find us block, FAQ and contact card until confirmed.
  • Prices. None published anywhere. A "20% off all services" reel title was seen but its date and validity are unknown, so no promotion is shown. Add a price list only if you want one on the site.
  • Online booking. The Fresha listing states the shop is not partnered with Fresha, so it is not linked as a booking page. Tell us if you take bookings online anywhere, and whether walk-ins are welcome.
  • Service list. Taken from the Fresha category list (no durations or prices). Waxing and tinting appear only in a YellowPages/Canpages snippet; salt scrubs, hot stone and paraffin appear only in a Wheree description that looks review-derived. All are held back pending your confirmation.
  • Group bookings. Shown because your own reels and posts invite them. If there are minimums, deposits or a group rate, they can go on the card and in the FAQ.
  • Reviews. The 4.5 rating and 576 count are what Birdeye displays for Google; Apple Maps shows 89% from 19 ratings. Yelp could not be read, so it is a link only. A lead note mentioned about 76 reviews on BestProsInTown, but no such page could be found. The one quoted review is real and displayed publicly on Birdeye; swap it for one you would rather feature.
  • Email. No public business email was found on any listing, so none is shown. Confirm the address you want on the site, if any.
  • Owner and staff names. None are published by the shop itself. A directory lists first names that appear to come from customer reviews; they were deliberately not used. Tell us if you want anyone named.
  • Credentials and years. No licences, certifications or "since" claims are published by the shop, so none appear. The Alberta Corporations listing shows LK Nail BAR Ltd registered January 31, 2022, Active — that is the only date used, in the footer.
  • Name styling. The registry spells it "LK Nail BAR Ltd"; social pages use "LK NAIL BAR". This draft uses "LK Nail Bar" throughout. Say which you prefer.
  • Address and map. Directories list "1312 17 Ave SW A"; your own caption gives "1312 17 Ave South West". Unit A is shown but should be confirmed, along with the entrance and any parking advice. The map is a stylised sketch, not to scale.
  • Social pages. Two Facebook pages exist (lknailbar.calgary and LKNailBar); only the first is linked. The Instagram bio, Facebook bios, TikTok and X pages could not be read, so no follower counts are shown. Confirm which pages are official.
  • Photos. None are used; the hero and gallery are illustrations and labelled placeholders. Six tiles are ready for real shots of the front, the pedicure room, sets, art and a group.
  • Policies. Deposits, cancellations, walk-ins and lead times are not stated anywhere public. Steps 1 and 4 of "Your visit" carry pills until you fill them in.
  • Request form. The draft form has no backend. The live site needs a delivery route (a form service or an SMS relay) so requests really do reach the shop.
  • Draft banner. Remove the black banner and the footer draft line when the site goes live, and lift the noindex tag so search engines can find it.
