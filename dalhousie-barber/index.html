Draft notes

Before this goes live: things to confirm with the owner
  • PricesNo source publishes a price list, so every line on the board says “to confirm”. Old customer reviews mention prices from 2010 to 2015, but those are stale and third-party, so they were not used. Give us the current list and it goes on the board.
  • HoursYelp shows Thursday and Friday closing at 7 PM and Sunday at 4 PM; Fresha, Waze and BestProsInTown show 6 PM Monday to Friday; two other directories show 7 PM weekdays and 5 PM Sunday. Facebook hours need a login to see. Every source agrees on seven days a week. Confirm the real hours and holiday closures; the pills on the hours board and contact card come off.
  • Unit number and postal codeFacebook, Yelp and 411.ca say #7, T3A 1Z6. Fresha, Waze and BestProsInTown say #20, T3A 2E2. The page uses #7 with a pill. Confirm which is right, and where in the centre the door is, so the map can be made real.
  • Payment methodsYelp lists Apple Pay and debit cards; BestProsInTown and a canada247 review say cash only, which looks out of date. Confirm cash, debit, credit and Apple Pay so the “pay and go” step and FAQ can be definite.
  • Established yearYelp’s “From the business” says established 1971 and notes the ownership change; Facebook says “nearly 53 years”. The two agree, but neither is independently verified. Confirm 1971 for the sign, and whether to mention when Abe took over.
  • Service listHaircut, haircut-and-shave and shave-only come from Yelp; men’s haircut, beard trim, head shave and hot towel shave come from a Fresha directory entry that says the shop is not affiliated with Fresha. Confirm the final list for the board — and whether kids’ cuts, seniors’ rates or anything else belong on it.
  • EmailThe Facebook page lists a hotmail address that looks personal. It is not printed on this draft; confirm it, or give us a shop address, before anything is published.
  • PhotosNone are available publicly. The hero scene and the four tiles in “The shop” are illustrations and labelled placeholders. Four phone photos — storefront, chairs, a finished cut, the barbers — replace them.
  • Barbers, chairs, languagesOnly Abe’s first name is published (Yelp owner blurb). Number of barbers and chairs, other staff names and languages spoken are not listed anywhere. Tell us and the “Who will cut my hair?” answer becomes specific.
  • Appointments and online bookingYelp says walk-ins welcome. No booking link exists; the Fresha page is a directory entry only. Confirm whether phone appointments are taken and whether you want online booking on the live site.
  • ReviewsThe Yelp figure (4.4 from 25 reviews) and two short quotes are shown with their sources. The Yelp listing is marked “Unclaimed” — claiming it is free and worth doing. Aggregator scores (BestProsInTown, Barberhead) are mentioned in a footnote only. The Bing local panel could not be verified from here.
  • Web address and socialNo shop-owned website was found; dalhousiebarbershop.ca/.com and dalhousiebarber.ca/.com could not be checked from this network. The Facebook page’s last visible post is from May 2020 and no Instagram was found. Pick a domain, and decide whether Facebook stays linked.
  • Name and spellingListings use “Dalhousie Barber Shop”; the Facebook page is “Dalhousie Barbershop”. This draft uses two words. Confirm the spelling for the sign, the title and the domain.
  • Map and landmarksThe sketch map’s cross road, transit line, stop and green space are unnamed placeholders, not taken from the shop’s listings, and the Landmarks line is flagged with a pill until you tell us the nearest cross-street and transit stop. The “Dalhousie Co-op Centre” name in the location text comes from a single directory (411.ca) and does not confirm a Co-op is there today. Yelp’s amenities list also shows “1 more attribute” that was not expanded, and the Foursquare, yably and cylex pages were login-walled.
  • Form, banner and go-liveThe contact form has no backend; the live site needs a delivery route so messages reach the shop. Remove the brass draft banner and the footer draft line when the site goes live. Once you confirm the year, staff experience and hours, the small “per Yelp / per Facebook” source lines come off and the figures stand on their own.
