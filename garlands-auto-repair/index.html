Draft notes

Before this goes live: things to confirm with the owner

Everything on this page comes from public directories, because Garland's has never published a website or a description of its own. That is the opportunity. Each dashed pill above marks a fact the owner can settle in a five-minute conversation.

  • Closing time. Directories agree on weekdays from 8:00 AM and closed weekends, but split between 5:00 and 5:30 PM, and one lists Friday differently. Pills appear in the hero card, hours table, FAQ, contact card and footer.
  • Years in business. BBB records a start of December 29, 1997; an earlier note about a City of Calgary licence since 2001 could not be verified. The page attributes 1997 to BBB everywhere it appears. Confirm the year and it becomes a plain "since" line.
  • Services. Oil changes, tire rotations, brake work, tire repairs and electrical diagnostics come from a community-edited aggregator; "minor and major repairs" from a YellowPages snippet; towing and auto parts from Wheree tags. Nothing is business-authored. Every card is labelled with its source until you give us your list.
  • Towing. A single directory tag. The card and FAQ both mark it to confirm.
  • How a visit works. Appointments or drop-ins, written estimates, approval before work, diagnostic fee, payment methods, warranty, loaner or shuttle: all flagged in the three steps and the FAQ.
  • Email. None published anywhere. The contact card and footer leave the slot open.
  • Names and "owner-operated". Directories list a principal contact and reviewers refer to the owner by first name, but the business itself publishes no name, so the site names nobody and does not say "owner-operated". Both can go in with your permission.
  • Independent, single location, phone answered on-site. Assumed from directory data, not stated by the business; confirm. Wording elsewhere on the page is kept general until then.
  • Phone number. 403-277-3699 is confirmed by BBB, Wheree, Nextdoor, 411.ca, Waze and AutoTechIQ, and is the only number on this page. Hotfrog, 2findlocal and Nexdu also show 403-277-3691, and a second 411.ca profile shows 3698. Worth asking the directories to correct.
  • Neighbourhood, parking, stale addresses. Directories label the address Highland Park; the brief said Greenview; the page says both. Parking and drop-off are unknown. One directory shows an old SW address that should be corrected.
  • Reviews. The 4.9 figure is what Wheree and AutoTechIQ display as an aggregated Google rating; neither shows a clear count, so no count is stated. Yelp's page could not be read (its search title says 10 reviews) and a Bing card showing 5/5 is unverified, so both are left out. The one quote shown is a Nextdoor neighbour's recommendation, attributed as such. Real Google reviews can replace the links once the shop's Google listing is confirmed.
  • BBB. The profile shows 0 complaints closed in the last 3 years, which the page states. It also shows "Not BBB Accredited" and "Not Rated" (accreditation is a paid program), so no rating or accreditation is claimed.
  • Facebook. A page exists but sits behind a login wall, so it is linked in the footer with a pill until you confirm it is yours and active.
  • Photos. None available publicly. Four placeholder tiles wait for the storefront, a bay, the tool wall and the front counter. The hero illustration is drawn, not a photo.
  • Prices, certifications, warranties, payment methods, languages. None stated anywhere public. Add only what you want on the site.
  • Domain. No owned website exists, and an aggregator squats on a look-alike address that says it is not official. Registering a real domain for the shop is step one.
  • Quote form. No backend in this draft. The live site needs a delivery route (a form service or SMS relay) so requests reach the shop's phone or inbox.
  • Draft banner. Remove the yellow banner and the footer draft line when the site goes live.
