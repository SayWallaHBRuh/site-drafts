A traditional barber’s shave. A cool towel and a balm finish it. It takes longer than a shave at home — that is the point.

Draft notes

Before this goes live: things to confirm with the owner

Everything on this page came from public listings checked in September 2026. Where those listings disagree or say nothing, the page shows a to-confirm flag instead of a guess. This is the list of those flags.

  • Hours. Sources conflict. Ali’s own reply on Birdeye (Mon–Fri 9–7, Sat 9–5, Sun 11–5) matches the Fresha listing; Birdeye’s own display says Mon–Fri 9–6; the Westbrook Mall store page says Mon–Fri 8:30 to 8, Sat 9–6, Sun and holidays 11–5; YellowPages and Google snippets say weekdays until 8 pm. The page shows Ali’s version. Confirm by phone, including holidays.
  • Prices. None published anywhere, so every menu line and the pricing FAQ carry a flag. Send the list and they fill in.
  • Services. Only men’s haircut, beard trim, head shave and hot towel shave are listed (Fresha), plus haircut-and-shave from a Yelp snippet. Colour or highlights were not verified by any source and are not claimed. Whether the “Hair Styling” half of the name means women’s or all-gender services is left open in the menu and FAQ.
  • Booking and walk-ins. No booking link exists. The Fresha page is a directory listing that says the business is not affiliated with Fresha, so it is not linked as booking. Walk-ins versus appointments is flagged in step 1 and the FAQ.
  • Ali. The name comes only from a Birdeye owner reply. Role, a short bio, a photo, and whether other barbers should be named are all to confirm — the page says “ask for Ali” and nothing more.
  • Reviews. The 4.3 rating and 149 count were read directly from the shop’s Birdeye page, which is unclaimed and aggregates Google reviews; a search-result title showed 146. Yelp returned an access error, so no Yelp rating is shown. Hotfrog has no reviews. One review excerpt is quoted with its source; swap in others with the reviewers’ permission if you prefer.
  • Trading name. Directories say “Westbrook Hair Styling & Barber Shop”, the Westbrook Mall directory says “Westbrook Barbers”, and Instagram says “Westbrook Barbershop”. Pick one for the site, the sign and the search listings.
  • Website and domain. No owned website was found on any listing. westbrookhairstyling.ca / .com, westbrookbarbershop.ca / .com and westbrookbarbers.ca returned no DNS record at the time of checking — availability still needs a registrar check.
  • Email. None published. The contact card shows a flag rather than an address.
  • Years in business. Not stated on any source, so there is no “since” line anywhere on the page. Supply the year and it goes in the hero.
  • Photos. No photos are used. The hero and the four gallery tiles are illustrations and say so. Phone photos of the storefront, chairs and finished cuts (with permission) replace them.
  • Address details. Every source agrees on Unit 32 – 1200 37 Street SW, T3C 1S2, except Hotfrog, which lists an odd T2G 2M3 postal code; the page uses T3C 1S2. Parking and the nearest mall entrance are flagged. The map is stylised from general knowledge of the area, not surveyed.
  • Payment and gift cards. Not stated anywhere public; flagged in step 3. Add only if you want them on the site.
  • Social. Instagram (@westbrook_barbershop, 56 followers, no website in the bio) is linked. No Facebook page was found — results were other Westbrook salons or an unrelated business elsewhere. Confirm whether one exists, and add the site link to the Instagram bio once live.
  • Request form. The draft form has no backend. The live site needs a delivery route — a form service or an SMS relay — so requests really reach the shop.
  • Draft banner. Remove the gold banner at the top and the draft line in the footer when the site goes live.
