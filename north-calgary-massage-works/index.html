Treatments

Five ways to let go

The treatments below are the ones the clinic lists today. Each description explains the modality itself — what it is and who tends to book it — not a promise about outcomes.

Therapeutic

Deep tissue massage

Slow, firm pressure that works into the deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue. Often chosen for stubborn tightness in the shoulders, back and hips, or after long days at a desk or on your feet.

Relaxation

Relaxation & Swedish massage

Long, flowing strokes and gentle kneading at a lighter pressure. The classic full-body massage for easing everyday stress, settling the nervous system and simply feeling rested.

Warmth

Hot stone therapy

Smooth, heated stones are placed on the body and used in the therapist’s hands. The warmth helps muscles soften more quickly, so the massage can go deeper with less effort.

Recovery

Sports & injury recovery

Focused work for active bodies — before an event, after a hard week of training, or while working back from a strain. Techniques are matched to the muscle groups you actually use.

Feet

Reflexology

Firm, specific pressure applied to points on the feet. Many people book it purely for how relaxing it is; it is also a good choice when you would rather stay fully clothed.
